📘 FAQ Assistant

Q: What’s “over the table” pay?

A: Wages reported to IRS with taxes withheld.

Q: Do I need a W-2 if I’m an S-Corp owner?

A: Yes, S-Corp owners must take a “reasonable” W-2 salary.

Q: What’s the risk of paying under the table?

A: IRS back taxes, penalties, audits, and loss of deductions.

Q: How do I estimate my payroll tax?

A: Input your federal, workers' comp, and ESD % on Step 1.
